Who I Am

A civil engineer focused on practical design and documentation.

I am a Licensed Civil Engineer with experience in engineering drafting, building plan preparation, structural analysis, cost estimation, and project implementation support.

My background includes work related to residential building plans, government infrastructure projects, quantity documentation, site inspections, and project coordination. These experiences have helped me understand both technical drawings and the practical requirements of project execution.

I use AutoCAD, SketchUp, Enscape, and STAAD.Pro in my workflow, while continuing to develop my capabilities in Revit and BIM documentation. I am interested in collaborating with engineering firms, contractors, architects, and clients who need reliable technical drafting and design support.
